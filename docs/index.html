PoseAnalysis

A local-first Codex skill that turns iPhone training videos into coach-grade Chinese reports for foil fencing, climbing, and youth fitness.

View on GitHub 中文说明 Demo reports

Evidence first

Every report starts with a video-quality gate and downgrades confidence when camera angle, occlusion, or landmark quality is weak.

Coach-facing

Reports focus on technical scoring, training prescriptions, next-session metrics, and same-template history comparison.

Private by default

Scripts process local videos and reports use anonymized child identity by default.

Professional Reference Base

PoseAnalysis is grounded in official sport rules, pose-estimation research, and youth training guidance. These sources shape terminology, scoring rubrics, and safety boundaries.
